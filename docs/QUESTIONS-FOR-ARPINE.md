# Questions for Arpine

Hello Arpine! The shop works. Some things on it can only come from you. These are facts and
decisions we must not invent, because the site would then promise something in your name. Each
question says why it matters and what the site shows today. A short answer is fine, in Armenian
or English, and you can answer them one at a time.

The first five matter **before we tell people about the shop**. The rest can follow.

---

### Before launch

**1. The postcard "Full series set": what does it cost, and how many cards are in it?**
- *Why:* buyers can choose "Full series set" on the postcards page, and it needs a real price.
- *Today:* the set shows **"Price confirmed by Arpine"** and is not counted in the total. Until
  this update it was charged **1,000 ֏, the price of one card**. A single card is still 1,000 ֏.

**2. Delivery: for each of the four choices, the real price, how long it takes, and who carries
it.**
- *Why:* the delivery price is added to the buyer's total, so it must be right.
- *Today* (all four are placeholders that we wrote, not your figures):

| Choice | Price | Time shown |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Collect in Yerevan | free | "Arrange a time when she confirms" |
| Courier in Yerevan | 1,500 ֏ | 1–2 days |
| Post within Armenia | 2,500 ֏ | 2–5 days |
| International post | 9,000 ֏ | 10–20 days, customs not included |

- Also: for international orders, who pays customs?

**3. How do buyers pay you?** For example: cash when they collect, bank transfer, Idram, a
card, or something else?
- *Why:* buyers want to know before they order.
- *Today:* the site says only "You pay only after her confirmation". It never says how.

**4. Returns, cancelling, and parcels damaged or lost in the post.**
- Can a buyer return or exchange an item? Within how many days, and who pays the postage back?
- Until when can a confirmed order be cancelled (for example, before it is made or posted)?
- If a parcel arrives damaged or is lost: do you replace it, refund it, or something else?
- *Why:* buyers expect this on the Terms page, and an order email might be disputed without it.
- *Today:* the Terms page says nothing about any of these. We left them out on purpose rather
  than guess.

**5. How should the seller be shown?**
- *Today:* the Terms page says **"Who sells: Arpine Baroyan, Yerevan, Armenia —
  arpenbaroyan@gmail.com"**.
- Is that right? Or do you want a business name, a registration number, or a phone number there?
  (See also question 11.)
